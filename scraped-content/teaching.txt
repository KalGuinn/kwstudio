Calling all Artists and Makers! It's time for a reset...
After mentoring dozens of teaching artists over the last 4 years, I've seen what it really takes to get traction in  your educator business.
I know so many of you are still struggling to figure out why things feel scattered and why your wheels are always spinning.

In this workshop we'll discuss the 5 blocks that are quietly holding you back and the one simple principle that will help your business grow.

In your session, I'll walk you through:

Why doing more doesn't fix the problem.

How being pulled in too many directions is quietly slowing your growth.

What it actually means to work within your Zone of Genius.

A simple framework I call the Rule of One, and why it changes everything.

Walk away with clarity and a new insights for growth on your terms.

You don’t need to figure everything out in this one workshop. But you will start to see what’s holding you back and how to start moving in the direction of your Big Audacious Dreams!
